Slide 2 · Problem Statement

Why Do We Need SafeStep?

🚨

Reactive vs. Proactive Gap

Traditional safety tools strictly focus on SOS alarms and location broadcasting after distress occurs, leaving the danger unprevented.

🌙

Nighttime Navigation Hazard

Women struggle to identify safer corridors after dark, where isolated shortcuts appear identical to safe arterial avenues on map tools.

💡

Infrastructure Deficits

Broken streetlights, secluded bus stops, unmonitored footpaths, and lack of nearby commercial activity multiply travel anxiety.

🧭

Shortest-Path Algorithmic Bias

Commercial navigation systems (Google Maps) optimize solely for minimum ETA and distance, ignoring pedestrian safety parameters.

🎯

Core Challenge: How can technology empower women to identify potential environmental risks before their journey begins and choose safer alternatives?

Slide 3 · Core Innovation

Time-Dependent Safety Intelligence

The standout innovation is our Time-Dependent Road Safety Engine: a road segment's risk dynamically changes based on hours, illumination, and pedestrian presence.

2:00 PM · Low Risk (🟢)

Broad daylight, active commercial storefronts, heavy pedestrian footfall, open public transit.

7:00 PM · Moderate Risk (🟡)

Dusk transitions, crowded transit junctions, varying streetlamp functionality, high vehicle traffic.

11:00 PM · High Risk (🔴)

Closed shops, sparse civilian movement, dark spots, isolated transit stops. Model penalizes this path.

📊

Data Transparency: SafeStep delivers transparent Confidence Levels and Data Freshness scores — recognizing that scores are intelligent estimates, not guarantees.

Slide 5 · Architecture

Technology Stack & 9 Modules

🛠️ Verified Technology Matrix

  • Frontend: React.js, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet.js
  • Backend: Python FastAPI (Async High Throughput)
  • Spatial Database: PostgreSQL + PostGIS (Geo-indexing)
  • Routing Engine: OSRM (Custom safety edge penalties)
  • Machine Learning: Python, Scikit-learn (Risk Regressor)
  • Computer Vision: YOLOv8 (Streetlight & defect audit)
  • Analytics & Auth: Chart.js & Firebase Auth

📦 The 9 System Modules

  • 1. User Registration & Authentication (Firebase)

Slide 6 · System Flow

Complete Project Workflow

Step 01 · Origin

User Request

User launches SafeStep, enters origin and destination coordinates.

Step 02 · Spatial Query

PostGIS Data Fetch

Extracts road segments, historical incidents, and ambient lighting buffers.

Step 03 · ML Scoring

Temporal AI Risk

Calculates dynamic road segment risk scores based on time and incidents.

Step 04 · OSRM Routing

Alternative Paths

OSRM balances safety vs. distance to generate recommended alternative paths.

Step 05 · Map Display

Visual Comparison

Shows Color-coded routes (🟢/🟡/🔴) on Leaflet with explainable tags.

Step 06 · Safe Shelters

En-Route Protection

Displays real-time police kiosks, hospitals, and 24/7 pharmacies along the path.

Step 07 · Journey Start

Tracking & SOS

Optional trusted-contact live tracking with instantaneous SOS panic button.

Step 08 · Civic Loop

Admin Analytics

Aggregates reported dark spots and broken lights for municipal repair teams.
